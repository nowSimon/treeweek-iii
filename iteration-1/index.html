What is this

A spell cast once a year
in the forest.

TREEWEEK is a self-organized week-long retreat, summer camp, and party for curious, creative minds. Music, art, philosophy, saunas, late-night fires, and the very real magic of many like-minded people finding each other.

It lives in the lineage of Vibecamp, Jesscamp, and Xiqweek — the European chapter of a growing community of friendly ambitious nerds who met on the internet and decided to Be together, in person, for a week.

"I always thought I'd have to get married to invite all my friends to one big garden party. Turns out you can just do things. How nice. : )"
— Simon Ohler, Organizer
