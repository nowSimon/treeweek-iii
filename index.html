it's main character season

DAY 1

Arrived. Got picked up from the train station. Had pumpkin soup and made myself a name bracelet from some beads. Met some people I'd known online for years — crazy, they really exist. Was tired from travel... early night for me.

DAY 2

Wow, food here is good. Had amazing breakfast and lunch, feeling nourished. People started doing workshops in the afternoon, I went to a "smelling workshop"? Crazy what you find out about people when you smell stuff together. Gonna check out the sauna tonight.

DAY 3

Went for a walk in the woods, got a massage from a bodyworker. We're friends now. Great fun cooking dinner for everyone. A bunch of stuff happened today but I can't remember. Just flowing at this point.

DAY 4

Apparently some people here are winemakers and they brought their own wine? And a Swedish guy has been creating some fermented drink in the basement. Good stuff. I joined some people on a jam and we were super in sync and busted out some crazy tunes in F# minor.

DAY 5

Today everyone felt super relaxed for some reason! We cleaned the house for a big party tonight. Other than that people were kind of just chilling around. I was in the hammock on the porch while someone got a haircut while playing the violin. She played so beautifully I burst out laugh-crying.

DAY 6
DAY 6

We threw a banger party last night. A bunch of my new friends were DJing, I didn't even know they could do that? I did a lot of chilling and chatting this week, it was good to party hard. Woke up in time to make it to the tree planting ceremony. So cute.

DAY 7

Wow, it's already time to go. What a crazy timeline I got into here. I'm gonna miss those people... glad I'm gonna stay in Berlin for another week to TREEcompress.

Damn... this TREEWEEK was a blast. I hope I can come again next year. It was a whole week but it still felt a bit too short. What if it was just one day longer?
